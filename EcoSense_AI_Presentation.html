Innovation

What Makes EcoSense Different

🔬

Predictive, Not Reactive

Unlike traditional BMS that react to current state, EcoSense anticipates demand 30 minutes ahead using LSTM models — preventing waste before it occurs.

🔧

Retrofit-Friendly Design

No rewiring needed. Sensors clip onto existing infrastructure, communicate wirelessly, and control existing HVAC through IR blasters or relay modules.

💰

India-Affordable BOM

Total hardware cost under ₹5,000 per zone — a fraction of imported BMS systems. Uses readily available Indian-market components.

🌞

Comfort-Aware Optimization

Energy savings never come at the cost of occupant comfort. The AI balances efficiency with thermal comfort indices (PMV/PPD) in real time.

Roadmap & Team

Implementation Roadmap

Phase 1 — Prototype

Months 1–3: Build sensor nodes, firmware, and basic dashboard. Validate in lab environment.

Phase 2 — Pilot

Months 4–6: Deploy in 1 real building (50+ zones). Collect training data, gather feedback.

Phase 3 — AI Refinement

Months 7–9: Train LSTM models on live data. Tune anomaly thresholds, optimize comfort model.

Phase 4 — Scale-Up

Months 10–12: Multi-building rollout. Publish whitepaper. Seek ECBC / BEE partnerships.
